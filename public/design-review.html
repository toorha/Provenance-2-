FAQ

Why not ChatGPT or Claude?›

General assistants have no persistent record of your property. Vera answers from the property’s own history, and shows the sources behind every answer.

Do teams have to change how they work?›

No. Vera works where the work already happens: email, meetings, documents. Capture is a side effect of normal activity.

What transfers when a property is sold?›

The property’s record: decisions, rationale, evidence and open issues. That is the point of a memory layer attached to the asset rather than to the team.
